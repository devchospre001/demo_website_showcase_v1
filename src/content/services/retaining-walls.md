## Walls that hold, and look good doing it

A retaining wall is mostly what you don't see: a proper footing, drainage stone behind every course and a drain pipe to carry water away. Skip those and the wall bows within a few years.

## Where walls help

- Leveling a sloped yard to make room for a patio
- Raised planting beds that keep roots out of the lawn
- Seat-height walls around a fire pit or patio edge

## Materials

Segmental concrete block for strength and value, or natural stone and shellstone caps for a softer, local look. Walls over the county height limit are engineered and permitted.
