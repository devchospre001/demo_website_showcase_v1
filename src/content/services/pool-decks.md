## A deck you can walk on at noon

Travertine and shellstone reflect heat instead of soaking it up, so your deck stays comfortable barefoot through a July afternoon.

## New decks and overlays

- **New decks** for new pools, coordinated with your pool builder
- **Paver overlays** over sound concrete decks, with new coping to match
- **Coping replacement** in travertine or shellstone bullnose

## Built for chlorine and salt

We use salt-resistant sealers and set every coping piece on mortar, not sand, so the pool edge stays put. We build decks only. For pool construction or repair, we'll recommend a builder we trust.
