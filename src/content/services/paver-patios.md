## A patio you'll use every evening

Most patios fail from the bottom up. We dig out, grade for drainage and compact six inches of base in layers before a single paver goes down. That's why our patios don't sink or hold puddles after the first summer storm.

## Materials we recommend

- **Travertine** stays cool underfoot and looks better with age. Our first choice around pools and seating areas.
- **Shellstone** is the local look: soft, sandy tones with visible shell texture.
- **Concrete pavers** cost less and come in dozens of shapes and colors. Ideal for driveways and large areas.

## What's included

Layout and 3D design, excavation and haul-off, compacted base, edge restraints, polymeric sand joints and a penetrating sealer. We finish with a walkthrough and care instructions.
