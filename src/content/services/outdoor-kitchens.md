## Cook, eat and stay outside

An outdoor kitchen works when it's planned around how you cook. We design the counter runs, landing space and seating first, then choose the appliances and materials to match.

## What we build

- Stone- or stucco-clad kitchen islands with built-in grills, side burners and refrigerators
- Pergolas and shade structures that handle Florida wind loads
- Wood-burning and gas fire pits, fire tables and outdoor fireplaces
- Bar and dining counters with seating for four to twelve

## Designed in 3D first

You see the whole space, with your appliances in place, before we break ground. One written price covers the design, the build and the utility rough-ins we coordinate.
